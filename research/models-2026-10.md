# Models beyond what we use (October 2026)

Written 2026-10-23. The question: which models, besides the ones Visualia
already runs, would help students make web pages, pictures and posters, 3D,
sound, video and voice pieces, with Estonian in mind. Four places to get them:
Workers AI, Cloudflare AI Gateway to other providers, OpenRouter and direct
APIs, and local machines (the pool and the tab).

What we use today, for reference: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (page model), Gemma 4
26B (fallback), FLUX.2 klein 4B (pictures), Clef-flash (decide), Ollama on the
pool's Macs, WebLLM in tabs (`HANDOFF.md`, `packages/models/models.json`).
Already measured elsewhere: local images on the M1 (`plans/local-images.md`),
models in the browser (`browser-models.md`), embeddings (`embeddings.md`).

**How this was checked:**
- Cloudflare: the unified model catalog
  ([developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models/),
  262 models), each model's page, the Workers AI
  [pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/)
  (updated 2026-10-20) and [limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/limits/)
  (updated 2026-09-17) pages, all read 2026-10-23.
- OpenRouter: its public models API (`/api/v1/models`, with
  `output_modalities=transcription|speech|image`) and `/api/v1/videos/models`,
  read 2026-10-23.
- Local: project READMEs, model cards and release notes; where a speed comes
  from a blog or a third party, it says so.
- Nothing here was run by us. Prices are list prices in USD.

**Model page links:** every Cloudflare id below has a page at
`https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models/<id>/`, for example
[`@cf/deepgram/nova-3`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models/@cf/deepgram/nova-3/)
or [`google/veo-3.1-fast`](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai/models/google/veo-3.1-fast/).
Prices in the Cloudflare tables are copied from those pages.

## The short answer

- **Estonian read-aloud is now one gateway call away.** Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS
  (`google/gemini-3.8-flash-tts`) is in Cloudflare's catalog, and Google lists
  Estonian for it. ElevenLabs Eleven v3 (`elevenlabs/eleven-v3`) also does
  Estonian. Workers AI's own voices (MeloTTS, Aura) still don't.
- **Estonian speech to text: the best is outside Workers AI.** ElevenLabs
  Scribe v2 (on OpenRouter, or ElevenLabs direct) and Deepgram Nova-3 (which
  added Estonian) beat Whisper by a wide margin. Workers AI's Whisper large-v3
  turbo is the cheap fallback. Locally, TalTech's Estonian Whisper fine-tune
  runs in whisper.cpp.
- **Video is now in the same catalog as text.** About 40 video models sit
  behind `env.AI.run()` with unified billing: Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.x, Wan 3.0,
  Kling (OpenRouter only), Pruna P-Video from $0.005 per second.
- **Music too:** ElevenLabs Music v2 ($0.0025 per second) and MiniMax Music 2.6
  ($0.15 per track) through the gateway; ACE-Step 1.5 (MIT, under 4 GB)
  locally.
- **Paying with AI Gateway credits raises our rate limit.** Paid-only Workers
  AI models (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash among them) get 50 requests a minute instead
  of 20 when billed through credits. For a class of twenty, that matters more
  than any new model.
- **Licences decide local video.** MiniMax H3 and HunyuanVideo 1.5 exclude the
  EU. Wan 2.2 (Apache 2.0), LTX-2.5 (free under $10M revenue) and Kandinsky 6.0
  Video Lite (MIT) are usable here.

## 2. Through AI Gateway to other providers

**Two ways in** ([unified billing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/features/unified-billing/),
updated 2026-09-30; [binding](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/usage/worker-binding-methods/)):

- **Catalog models with unified billing.** `env.AI.run("google/veo-3.1-fast", …,
  { gateway: { id } })`, or the REST endpoints `/ai/run`,
  `/ai/v1/chat/completions`, `/ai/v1/responses`, `/ai/v1/messages`
  ([REST API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/usage/rest-api/)).
  No provider keys. Provider prices pass through; credits carry a 5% fee.
  Logging, caching, rate limits, spend limits per gateway or metadata
  ([spend limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/features/spend-limits/)).
  Some models are marked "Zero data retention".
- **Provider-native endpoints with our own key (BYOK).** Anthropic, Azure
  OpenAI, Baseten, Bedrock, Cartesia, Cerebras, Cohere, Deepgram, DeepSeek,
  ElevenLabs, fal, Google AI Studio, xAI, Groq, Hugging Face, Ideogram,
  Mistral, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Parallel, Perplexity, Replicate, Vertex
  ([providers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/usage/providers/)).
  fal, for example, is `https://gateway.ai.cloudflare.com/v1/{account}/{gateway}/fal`
  in place of `https://fal.run` ([fal](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/usage/providers/fal/)).
  Usage is still logged in the gateway.

Third-party providers in the catalog (2026-10-23): Alibaba, Anthropic,
AssemblyAI, Black Forest Labs, Bria, ByteDance, ElevenLabs, Fireworks,
Google, Inworld, Krea, Lightricks, Meta, MiniMax, Moonshot, OpenAI, PixVerse,

## Licence traps

- **EU excluded:** MiniMax H3, HunyuanVideo 1.5 (and other Tencent Hunyuan
  models). EKA is in the EU.
- **Non-commercial:** Qwen Image 2.1, FLUX.2 klein 9B, FLUX 3 Image Klein
  (non-commercial for everyone; commercial output only for under-$5M users,
  with output filtering and AI disclosure). A course is non-commercial; a
  student selling a poster is not.
- **Revenue-capped:** LTX-2.5 (under $10M), Stable Audio 3 (Community).
- **Copyleft engines:** Piper and `draw-things-cli` are GPL-3.0; run them as
  separate programs, as the worker app already does with Ollama.
- **Provider terms:** every gateway model links its own terms on its
  Cloudflare page; "Zero data retention" is marked per model.

## Recommendations for Visualia

1. **Switch the gateway's Workers AI billing to AI Gateway credits.** Paid
   models go from 20 to 50 requests a minute, and third-party models come on
   the same balance ([limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/limits/),
   [changelog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-08-07-workers-ai-unified-billing/)).
   Gateway.
2. **Estonian read-aloud: `google/gemini-3.8-flash-tts`.** Estonian listed,
   steerable, through `env.AI.run`; TartuNLP Neurokõne as the free Estonian
   alternative; Piper `et_EE` in the tab. Studio feature, gateway.
3. **Estonian speech to text: ElevenLabs Scribe v2** (OpenRouter or ElevenLabs
   BYOK through the gateway), Whisper large-v3 turbo on Workers AI as the
   cheap fallback, TalTech's Estonian Whisper in the pool. Spoken briefs,
   voice pieces.
4. **Edit pictures with what we have.** FLUX.2 klein 4B already takes input
   images on Workers AI ($0.000059 per input tile); add "change this
   picture" before adding models. FLUX 3 Image Klein (`black-forest-labs/flux-3-image-klein`,
   $0.009/MP, 10 references, layout boxes) for posters, tested with Estonian
   letters. Studio, gateway.
5. **SVG from Recraft V4.1 vector** ($0.08). Logos, icons and poster parts
   that live in the page as code, not pixels. Studio, gateway.
6. **Video in two tiers.** `pruna/p-video` drafts ($0.005/s at 720p) for
   everyone; `google/veo-3.1-fast` with sound ($0.10/s) or
   `bytedance/seedance-2.0-mini` for finals, behind a per-person budget.
   Studio, gateway.
7. **Music: `elevenlabs/music-v2`** ($0.0025/s) in the studio; **ACE-Step
   1.5** (MIT, under 4 GB) as the pool's music job. Sound pieces.
8. **A cheaper page-model route to test:** DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on OpenRouter
   at half the Workers AI price, and `@cf/zai-org/glm-5.3-flash` ($0.15/$0.50,
   multimodal, 1M context) as a second fallback. Measure Estonian with our
   probes first; EuroEval hasn't ranked either. Gateway.
9. **One image and video engine for the worker app: stable-diffusion.cpp**
   (MIT; Metal, CUDA, Vulkan; FLUX.2 klein, Z-Image, Wan 2.2, LTX-2.5).
   GeForce lab PCs: Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B and LTX-2.5 in 8–16 GB; Draw Things for
   students who want a GUI on their own Mac. Pool.
10. **Voice cloning to explore: OmniVoice** (Apache 2.0, Estonian in its
    training data). The only open cloning model found that claims Estonian;
    test it on the M2 before offering it. Pool; a lesson on consent and
    voices.
