# Curiosity and play

Part of `README.md`. Gathered 2026-11-11. *Speculation* and *from memory* mark what was not checked.

Curiosity is valuable before anyone knows what it will produce. A long line of
thinkers on materials, rules, chance and feedback; details and links in
`sources/synthesis.md` section 3.

- Fröbel's blocks.
- Dewey, *Art as Experience* (1934): doing and undergoing.
- Cage: chance and indeterminacy.
- Papert and Harel, *Situating Constructionism* (1991); Kafai's game-making.
- Reggio Emilia: the hundred languages.
- Resnick's spiral: imagine, create, play, share, reflect.
- Gopnik, *Childhood as a Solution to Explore-Exploit Tensions* (2020): childhood as
  protected exploration.
- Boden: exploring a conceptual space versus transforming it.
- **Arvo Pärt**, tintinnabuli (1976): a new language found through constraint and
  listening. Freedom as choosing constraints, not maximising output.
- Papert, *Mindstorms* (1980): computers as materials to think with. Making helps only
  with understanding, testing and reflection; "any making equals learning" is a
  flattening.
- Resnick, *Lifelong Kindergarten* (2017): projects, peers, passion, play. Making for
  yourself is legitimate learning.
