# HARNO: digital competence, AI, work and making

Research gathered in November 2026 for a talk at HARNO's digital competence conference
*"Raamistikust klassiruumi"* (27.11.2026). The question underneath: when making things
becomes easy, what becomes hard, and what does school do about it?

The tensions, in one list (from `sources/synthesis.md`):

- AI expands what one person can make, but making is not learning.
- Play is worth it before a purpose is known, but expertise still grows through practice
  and feedback.
- Solitude can enable exploration; isolation takes away response and community.
- New tools spread production; ownership and governance decide how much agency users have.
- Young people have more creative power, and fewer sure routes into some professions.
