# The maker's material

Part of `README.md`. What the talk can draw on from positron and Visualia.

- **Lonely Software**, the essay: `~/personal/positron/demo/lonely-software/index.html`;
  the deck: https://positron.studio/slides/lonely/ (`lonely` in
  `positron/demo/shell/decks.mjs`). The 1988 notebooks, one person making worlds inside
  worlds, the lonely node, the loopback, the ZKM sphere, "But who is listening?", "Just the
  possibility to see what happens next."
- **Loopback demos**: the camera at its own monitor, https://positron.studio/devices/hdmi/ ;
  the drone into its own echoes, https://positron.studio/ambient/drone/ (microphone open);
  plan in `positron/plans/plan-feedback-demo.md`.
- **The EKA course**, `schools/eka/docs/COURSE-CARD.md`: *"Uudishimulik tehisintellekt:
  mängides kohalike mudelitega"*. Open models on EKA's own machines, every call seen and
  counted, students log model, time and energy. Measured: about 0.32 Wh for a 1024² image
  locally (`plans/local-images.md`), about $0.0014 a page on a cheap cloud model
  (`worklog/2026-10.md`).
- **The class space** (`plans/class-space.md`): one class chat, everyone's work on the
  home page, peers change each other's projects with credit.
- **The backstage rule** (`plans/backstage.md`): logging stays; inference only when the
  student asks; the backstage is private by default; no teacher dashboard of process.
- **Estonian visuals** (`plans/estonian-visuals.md`): how little picture models know about
  Estonia. Only one open model got "Kevadnäitus 2027" right at both sizes
  (`worklog/2026-10.md`).
- **Value drift** (`schools/eka/docs/DRIFT.md`): the TI-Hüpe risk analysis read for an art
  school.
