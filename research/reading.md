## Defining the problems: ten ways LLMs break the traditional school system

1. **Homework is broken.** Assessment shifts from take-home tasks to live
   thinking, dialogue and reflection.
2. **Knowing is not learning.** When facts are instant, focus on analysis,
   synthesis and better questions.
3. **Teachers become mentors.** They teach discernment, not deliver content.
4. **Communication beats correctness.** Storytelling, persuasion, collaboration
   and listening over right answers.
5. **Creativity is redefined.** Cultivate imagination, risk-taking, emotion and
   personal voice, especially through the arts.
6. **Language class becomes culture class.** Humour, tone, metaphor and intent
   stay human.
7. **Emotional intelligence is essential.** Self-awareness, empathy, conflict
   resolution, connection.
8. **Subjects converge.** Interdisciplinary learning and creative projects.
9. **Motivation depends on meaning.** Restore purpose through growth, relevance
   and craftsmanship.
10. **Let school become human.** Protect and expand presence, intuition, ethics,
    art and love.

## Giving feedback: the sticky-dot reflection system

A non-competitive, analog way to grade. Students place coloured dots on a
personal wall, chart or notebook. Each colour is a kind of learning moment. No
scores, no rankings, just patterns over time, with a weekly or monthly
reflection.

| Colour | Art / music / drama | Science | Math | Language & literature | Social studies / civics |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow | I felt inspired or moved | I asked a curious question | I noticed a pattern | I connected deeply with a text or theme | I saw a new point of view |
| Blue | I completed something meaningful | I figured out how something works | I solved a problem clearly | I expressed myself clearly | I explained my thoughts clearly |
| Red | I hit a block but kept going | I made a mistake and learned | I was stuck but didn't give up | I struggled but kept trying | I faced something uncomfortable |
| Green | I supported someone else's creative process | I helped someone understand | I explained my thinking to a classmate | I listened or read with full attention | I listened deeply or helped resolve a conflict |
| Purple | I tried something bold or unfamiliar | I tried something unusual | I found a new strategy | I took a creative risk | I imagined a better system |

What progress looks like:
- Art, music and drama: expressive depth, risk-taking, artistic growth.
- Science: curiosity → understanding → experimentation.
- Math: deeper thinking, flexible strategies, confidence.
- Language and literature: confidence, emotional resonance, owning one's voice.
- Social studies and civics: perspective shift, moral imagination, systems
  thinking.

Reflection prompts:
- "Which colour showed up most this week?"
- "What dot would you add for today?"
- "What changed about your dots over the last month?"
- "What colour do you wish you had more of?"
- "Is there a dot you placed that surprised you?"
